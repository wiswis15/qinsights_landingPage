PDF files often contain articles, reports, and other reference material, but can also hold interview or focus group transcripts.

## Uploading a PDF

The workflow mirrors [Word documents](/docs/word-documents):

- **Transcripts:** select **Detect Speakers** when uploading. See [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting) for how to format speaker IDs so they're recognized reliably.
- **Articles, reports, and notes:** click **Upload New File**, select the PDF, and click **Proceed** — there's nothing to transcribe or detect.

## Where PDFs are used

PDF is supported across most of QInsights' analysis modes:

- [Theme Analysis](/docs/theme-analysis)
- [Grid Analysis](/docs/grid-analysis)
- [Conversational Analysis](/docs/conversational-analysis)
- [Guided Conversational Analysis](/docs/guided-analysis)

## Next steps

Once uploaded, configure [Data Context](/docs/data-context) — assign speaker roles for transcripts, or add document characteristics (source, year, type) for reports and articles — before selecting the file for analysis.
