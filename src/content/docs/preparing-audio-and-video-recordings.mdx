Supported formats are:

- **Audio:** `.mp3`, `.m4a`
- **Video:** `.mp4`
- **Maximum size:** 1 GB

There's nothing further to prepare on your end - QInsights transcribes the file for you once it's uploaded. See [Transcribing Audio or Video Files](/docs/transcribing-audio-or-video-files) for that workflow, including the custom dictionary and reviewing the resulting transcript.
