Your data is protected by leading security standards. It is **never used to train AI models**, and the servers QInsights uses are compliant with major privacy standards, including GDPR, ISO/IEC 27701, and HIPAA.

For a full breakdown of infrastructure, sub-processors, and data handling, see the dedicated [privacy policy](/privacy) and [compliance page](/compliance). This page covers the account-level security controls available inside the app.

## Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

You can enable Multi-Factor Authentication in **Settings → Security** as an extra layer of protection for your account.

## Automatic logout after inactivity

For security reasons, QInsights automatically logs users out after **30 minutes of inactivity**. If you plan to step away for a longer period - for lunch or a meeting, for example - your session may expire while you're away. When you return, you may need to log in again before continuing your work.

## Preparing sensitive data before upload

If your source material contains personally identifiable information you'd rather not upload as-is, use the [Data Anonymizer Tool](/docs/data-anonymizer) to strip or mask names, contact details, and other identifiers locally, before anything reaches QInsights.
