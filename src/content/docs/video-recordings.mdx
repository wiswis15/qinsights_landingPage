Video files follow the same transcription workflow as [audio recordings](/docs/audio-recordings).

- **Supported format:** `.mp4`
- **Maximum size:** 1 GB
- QInsights currently transcribes **one file at a time**

## Uploading a video file

1. From **Upload New File**, select your video file.
2. Choose **Auto-detect** or a preset language for better transcription accuracy.
3. Optionally add terms to the [custom dictionary](/docs/audio-recordings#improve-accuracy-with-a-custom-dictionary-optional) before transcription starts, then click **Start transcription**.

Once transcribed, the video stays linked to its transcript - when you review or edit the text later, the video plays alongside the corresponding segment, exactly as with audio.

## Reviewing and editing

Use the same editing tools described in [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting#checking-and-editing-transcripts) - click a time mark to jump to that point in the recording, double-click text to correct it, and use the **Speakers** tab to adjust names. Playback controls let you jump in 10-second intervals and adjust speed (0.75x–2x).

## Next steps

Once your transcript is reviewed, open [Data Context](/docs/data-context) to assign speaker roles and define characteristics.
