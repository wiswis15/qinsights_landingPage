QInsights can transcribe audio and video files directly, so you can work with spoken data - interviews, focus groups, recorded conversations - without preparing a transcript in advance.

Select your file from the file browser as you would any other data source, then choose **Auto-detect** or one of 18 preset languages - selecting a specific language improves transcription accuracy. The **Custom Dictionary** dialog opens automatically before transcription begins.

> **Note:** QInsights currently transcribes one audio or video file at a time.

## Add Terms to a Dictionary to Improve Accuracy (Optional)

Before transcription begins, you can add custom terms - company or brand names, acronyms, medical or other domain-specific terminology - so the transcription tool spells them correctly.

- Add single terms one at a time in the editor, then click **Add terms**.
- Or prepare a text or Excel file with one term per line and click **Choose File** to import the list.
- If you don't need this, click **Skip dictionary**.

Review the selected file and dictionary terms on the next screen, then click **Start transcription**.

When transcription completes, the Data Context panel opens automatically so you can assign a role to each detected speaker. At this stage you can't yet tell who is the respondent and who is the interviewer, since speakers are still labeled generically (Speaker A, Speaker B). It's usually easier to close Data Context first, check and correct the transcript as described below, and only then reopen Data Context to assign roles.

## Checking and Editing Transcripts

Once transcription is complete, open the three-dot menu (⋮) on the document card for these options:

| Option | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Preview** | Opens a read-only view of the transcript |
| **Rename** | Renames the document within QInsights |
| **See Speakers** | Shows the list of detected speakers |
| **Edit** | Opens the transcript alongside the audio or video, so you can review and correct the text and adjust speaker names |

<figure className="docs-article__figure">
  <img className="docs-article__inline-image" src="/docs/25.jpg" alt="Reviewing and editing a transcript alongside its audio recording." />
  <figcaption className="docs-article__figcaption">Reviewing and editing a transcript alongside its audio recording.</figcaption>

In edit mode:

- Click a time mark to jump playback to that point.
- Double-click inside the text to correct it.
- Open the **Speakers** tab to rename detected speakers - names are saved for the transcript and reused across analysis and references.

<figure className="docs-article__figure">
  <img className="docs-article__inline-image" src="/docs/27.png" alt="Renaming detected speakers before assigning roles in Data Context." />
  <figcaption className="docs-article__figcaption">Renaming detected speakers before assigning roles in Data Context.</figcaption>
</figure>

Playback controls let you jump ±10 seconds and adjust speed (0.75x-2x). Video files work exactly the same way - the video plays alongside the transcript as you review it.

Once the speaker names are correct, open **Data Context** to define who is the respondent and who is the interviewer/moderator.
