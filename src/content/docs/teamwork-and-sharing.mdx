QInsights lets you share a project with colleagues - useful when working in a research team or getting a second opinion on your analysis. Project sharing works for any QInsights account and isn't limited to [team licences](/docs/settings-and-billing#admin-settings-and-team-licences).

## Sharing a Project

1. Click **Share Project** at the top left of your project space.
2. Enter the email address your teammate uses for their QInsights account and click **Share Project**. They'll receive an email notification with access details.

## What Shared Teammates Can Do

Once shared, a teammate can:

- see every analysis saved in the [Analysis Archive](/docs/analysis-archive)
- run new analyses and add results to the project
- interact with the data just as you would

To protect the integrity of your dataset, invited teammates **cannot delete documents** from the project.

This makes it straightforward to divide work across a team and keep analysis consistent across a shared project.
