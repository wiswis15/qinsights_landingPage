## Troubleshooting

### An analysis or import is stuck at 0%

Refresh the page using the reload button next to your browser's address bar.

### I got an error message during analysis or import

This is often a brief internet connection interruption rather than an app issue. Wait a few seconds and try again. If the error persists, it may be a bug - please [contact support](#support) so it can be investigated.

### No preview appears after uploading a document

The upload likely failed because the connection to the server was interrupted. Delete the document and upload it again. Avoid continuing to work with a document that has no summary preview, since this can lead to errors later - see [Uploading Files](/docs/uploading-files#if-a-summary-doesnt-appear).

### Q (the AI assistant) refuses to answer

This usually means your question triggered a language-model guardrail, which is outside QInsights' control. Rephrasing the question resolves this in most cases.

### I keep seeing repeated error messages

If errors persist across several actions, the system may no longer be responding correctly. Log out and log back in.

## General Questions

**Is my data used to train AI models?**
No. Your data is never used for training, and QInsights infrastructure is compliant with GDPR, ISO/IEC 27701, and HIPAA. See [Data Security and Privacy](/docs/security-and-privacy) and the full [privacy policy](/privacy).

**How long can I stay logged in without doing anything?**
QInsights automatically logs you out after 30 minutes of inactivity, for security reasons.

**Can I anonymize my data before uploading it?**
Yes - the [Data Anonymizer Tool](/docs/data-anonymizer) runs locally on your device and can mask or replace up to 27 categories of personal and sensitive information before anything reaches QInsights.

**Why does my analysis feel too general when I query a large dataset?**
Large language models tend to summarize broadly across large volumes of data, which can dilute nuance. Try narrowing your query to a smaller, targeted subset using [filters](/docs/filters) - see [Prompting Strategies](/docs/prompting-strategies#querying-all-data-vs-subsets) for why this helps and how to do it well.

**Can I combine Excel survey data with Word or PDF transcripts in one project?**
No - spreadsheet data is processed differently and can't be combined with Word, PDF, audio, or video files in a single analysis. Keep survey data in its own project; see [Excel and Survey Data](/docs/excel-survey-data).

**Why does Q sometimes answer in the wrong language?**
Unprompted analyses (Summary, Theme Analysis) follow the language of your source document; prompted analyses (Conversational Analysis and similar) follow the language of your prompt. If the response comes back in the wrong language, just ask a follow-up such as *"Please write the answer in German."* See [Introduction - Language support](/docs/introduction#language-support).

**What's the difference between highlighted references and formal coding?**
Highlights point to the general region of a document that supports an answer - sometimes only a fragment is highlighted. They're meant to help you validate Q's answer against your data, not to serve as precisely coded segments.

**Can someone I share a project with delete my documents?**
No - invited teammates can run analyses and add results, but they can't delete documents from a shared project. See [Teamwork and Sharing](/docs/teamwork-and-sharing).
