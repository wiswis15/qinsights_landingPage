Theme Analysis scans your data to identify recurring topics, ideas, and patterns. QInsights groups related content into themes, generates a title for each, and provides a short description supported by references back to your data.

Supported formats:

- **Word files** - typically interview or focus group transcripts
- **PDF files** - often articles and reports
- **Excel files** - ideal for open-ended survey responses, discussion boards, stakeholder feedback, or social media comments

## A Content-Mapping Approach, Not Coding

Theme Analysis gives you a **high-level content map** of your data - a starting point that highlights areas worth exploring further, not a finished thematic analysis. While the term "theme" may evoke thematic analysis approaches like Braun and Clarke's, in QInsights themes are data-driven insights that surface early to help you navigate your material. They're exploratory and pragmatic, designed to give you an actionable entry point while you stay in control of the direction and depth of the analysis.

## How It Works

QInsights analyzes your selected documents and identifies **2 to 10 themes**, each representing a significant topic or pattern, with a short description summarizing its relevance.

### Reviewing a theme (Word and PDF)

Click **Show more Details** on a theme to see its prevalence - how many documents it occurs in - and how many quotes support it.

## Interactive Exploration: Follow-up Chats

Once the themes are identified, you can ask follow-up questions about specific themes or explore the data further to refine your understanding and extract deeper insights. Thus, the themes identified by QInsights are not an endpoint but a starting point for analysis. They serve as a guide to help you:

- identify potential topics of interest to explore further
- prioritize areas for deeper investigation based on the prevalence of themes
- save time by quickly highlighting what your data is "about" before diving into more detailed analysis

To start a follow-up chat, click the Q icon at the bottom of the themes view - it opens a follow-up chat linked to that Theme Analysis in the [Analysis Archive](/docs/analysis-archive).

## The Value of Theme Analysis

The value of Theme Analysis extends beyond simple identification and exploration. With the insights gained from the initial themes, you can make informed decisions on how to allocate your research efforts effectively - delving deeper into highly prevalent themes, or exploring fewer common topics that could uncover unique insights.

Ultimately, Theme Analysis in QInsights is not just about understanding what your data contains; it's about strategically using that knowledge to drive your research forward. Whether you're conducting academic research, market analysis, or evaluating user feedback, the themes provide a robust foundation for developing deeper, actionable insights that can influence real-world decisions and strategies.

By mapping out the main themes and their connections, you can develop a comprehensive understanding of your data landscape, which empowers you to navigate through your data with precision and purpose. It equips you with a content map that is both a snapshot of your current data and a compass guiding your future research directions.
