Word (`.docx`) files are the most common format for interview and focus group transcripts, as well as reports, observational notes, and other written material.

## Two kinds of Word uploads

**Transcripts prepared elsewhere.** If your document is an interview or focus group transcript, select **Detect Speakers** when uploading so QInsights can identify who said what. See [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting) for the speaker-ID format QInsights recognizes reliably.

**Reports, notes, and other text.** For material that isn't a multi-speaker transcript - reports, observational notes, articles - click **Upload New File**, select the document, and click **Proceed** directly. There's nothing to transcribe or detect.

## After upload

Each uploaded file appears as a document card. Use the three-dot menu to **Preview**, **Rename**, or **See Speakers** (for transcripts, an **Edit** option is also available - see [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting#checking-and-editing-transcripts)).

If a document's **Summary** button appears faded, no summary was generated during import, usually due to a connection interruption during upload - delete and re-upload it rather than continuing to work with a file that has no summary. See [Uploading Files](/docs/uploading-files#if-a-summary-doesnt-appear).

## Next steps

For transcripts specifically, continue to [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting). For all Word documents, configure [Data Context](/docs/data-context) to add speaker roles or document characteristics before running an analysis.
