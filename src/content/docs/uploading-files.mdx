To add documents to your project, click **Upload New File**. You'll be asked to choose one of two upload paths, depending on what you're adding.

## Choosing the Right Upload Path

1. **Transcribing audio or video.** Choose **Auto-detect** or one of 18 preset languages for better transcription accuracy. See [Audio Recordings](/docs/audio-recordings) and [Video Recordings](/docs/video-recordings).
2. **Uploading a transcript prepared elsewhere.** Switch on **Detect Speakers**. See [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting) for how to format speaker IDs so QInsights can recognize them reliably.

For reports, notes, or articles in Word or PDF format, and for spreadsheet-based survey data, there's nothing to transcribe or detect - select the file and click **Proceed** directly. See [Word Documents](/docs/word-documents), [PDF Documents](/docs/pdf-documents), and [Excel & Survey Data](/docs/excel-survey-data) for format-specific preparation guidance.

> **Note:** QInsights currently transcribes one audio or video file at a time.

## After Upload: The Project View

Once your files are uploaded, each one appears as a **document card** showing basic information and quick actions (view a summary, delete, or open the three-dot menu for more options).

**Recommendation:** keep survey data stored in Excel files in a separate project. Excel data is processed differently from Word, PDF, audio, or video files and can't be combined with them in a single analysis. A separate project also keeps [Data Context](/docs/data-context) manageable, since spreadsheet columns are imported automatically and can quickly add up.

### If a summary doesn't appear

If the **Summary** button on a Word or PDF document card looks faded, no summary was generated during import - usually because the upload connection was interrupted. Delete the document and upload it again rather than continuing to work with a file that has no summary preview, as this can cause errors later. Excel summaries appear after a page reload.

### Three-dot menu

For Word, PDF, and Excel files, the three-dot menu on a document card offers:

- **Preview** - open a read-only view of the document
- **Rename** - give the document a name that fits your analysis
- **See Speakers** - view detected speakers (transcripts)

Transcripts have an additional option, **Edit**, for reviewing and correcting the transcript text - see [Transcript Formatting](/docs/transcript-formatting).

### Deleting documents

- **Single document:** click the bin icon on its card.
- **Multiple documents:** select their checkboxes, then click the bin icon in the action toolbar.

### Sorting documents

Use the sort controls above the file list to order document cards by:

- **Name (A–Z)** - click once for A→Z, again to reverse
- **Date (Newest/Oldest)** - click again to reverse the order

Sorting helps you find files quickly in larger projects.

## Next Steps

Once your files are in, configure [Data Context](/docs/data-context) - speaker roles and respondent or document characteristics - so QInsights can interpret your data correctly during analysis.
